Chuo-Sogo Procure user guide (trial 1.0.0)

Software that does a factory's buying work, with AI.

1. Install

  1. Save and open ChuoSogo-Setup.exe (about 300 MB) from the download page.
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose "More info" → "Run anyway" (the trial is not signed).
  3. Follow the installer and start Chuo-Sogo Procure.

2. First start: choose how to use it

  1. Connect to a server: the trial server. Use the role buttons on the sign-in screen (factory requester, buyer, approver, finance, our staff) to try each role. The trial server holds fictional sample data.
  2. Use on this PC only: everything runs on one PC. Enter the factory name and an admin email and password.
  3. In this-PC-only mode, install the additional data (about 10.8 GB): the high-accuracy AI for PCs with 16 GB of memory, the light AI for 8 GB. Downloads resume after interruptions and every file is checked by its SHA-256 fingerprint.

3. The workflow (follow the "where are we" guide on each request)

  1. Request: the factory says what it needs; paste text or a file and the AI reads the items (you confirm them).
  2. Identify: our staff confirm part numbers.
  3. Quote: the AI suggests about 10 suppliers with reasons; our staff check before the request goes out; remind suppliers who have not replied.
  4. Read replies: email, PDF, Excel and FAX replies are read by the AI into the quotation form; staff check and save.
  5. Compare and recommend: our staff check the comparison before the factory sees it.
  6. Approve and order: the factory approves and orders with its own PO number; delivery dates are followed and late orders flagged.
  7. Receive and invoice: goods receipt and supplier-invoice matching close it.

4. Useful pages

  1. Savings: how much each price went down and our fee (50% of the drop); no drop, no fee.
  2. Purchase history → Where you can save: items with the most room to save, from past records.
  3. Records: quotations, orders and invoices searchable by date, amount and counterparty (電子帳簿保存法); received files are kept as received.
  4. Mail inbox (our staff): assign replies and FAXes that could not be matched automatically.

5. Easier to read

  1. Settings → Reading comfort: text size (normal, large, extra large) and high contrast.
  2. Every button has a word on it, and actions that cannot be undone ask first.

6. About the trial

  1. Live email and FAX sending is switched off; messages are only recorded.
